# 💫 About Me

**👋 Hi there! I'm Pierre-Nikolas Contessotto**

I'm a Frenchman living in Tokyo, and the founder of **Yūgen**, a small atelier that helps French brands find their place in Japan. We work on branding, packaging, design, content, web & e-commerce, and market-entry strategy, always with the same idea in mind: France leads, Japan adapts.

Day to day, I move between three languages (French 🇫🇷, Japanese 🇯🇵, English 🇬🇧) and two cultures that don't always agree on what "good design" means. That's exactly what makes the work interesting. Most of my projects gravitate around premium food & beverage, wine, and artisan products. I'm also the Japan Ambassador for the **Université du Vin de Suze-la-Rousse** 🍷.

Lately I've been getting serious about **Python and data science** (NumPy, statistics, Jupyter notebooks) because I want AI to become part of how we design and analyze markets, not just a buzzword on a slide.

💬 Happy to chat about Franco-Japanese branding, wine, design, or what it actually takes to launch a product in Japan.

⚡ When I'm not working, I'm studying for my private pilot licence ✈️, or playing a game of [Age of Empires II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires_II).

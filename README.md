<h1 align="center">About Me</h1>

**Hi there! I'm Pierre-Nikolas Contessotto**

I'm a Frenchman living in Tokyo, and the founder of **Yūgen**, a small atelier that helps French brands find their place in Japan. We work on branding, packaging, design, content, web & e-commerce, and market-entry strategy, always with the same idea in mind: France leads, Japan adapts.

Day to day, I move between three languages (French 🇫🇷, Japanese 🇯🇵, English 🇬🇧) and two cultures that don't always agree on what "good design" means. That's exactly what makes the work interesting. Most of my projects gravitate around premium food & beverage, wine 🍷, and artisan products.

Right now I'm leveling up through **Le Wagon Tokyo's Data Science & Machine Learning bootcamp** (batch #2323) — Pandas, scikit-learn, deep learning, and shipping models to production with FastAPI and Docker — because I want AI to become part of how we design and analyze markets, not just a buzzword on a slide.

Happy to chat about Franco-Japanese branding, wine, design, or what it actually takes to launch a product in Japan.

When I'm not working, I'm studying for my private pilot licence ✈️.
